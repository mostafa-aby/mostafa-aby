## Mostafa Jafarian Abyaneh

PhD student in **Civil Engineering** at the **University of Maryland, College
Park**, working with Prof. Yunfeng Zhang in the Structural Engineering
Laboratory.

My work is in **computational solid mechanics**: constitutive models for
composite, brittle and soft materials, nonlinear finite element simulation of
how they fail, and machine learning and Bayesian optimization to calibrate
those models against experimental data.
